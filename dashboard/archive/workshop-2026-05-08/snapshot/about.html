Cultured Chicken Focus

The cultured chicken pivotal question asks:

What will be the production cost of cultured chicken by [target year], and what are the implications for animal welfare?

Warning

Model status (March 2026): This model is largely AI-generated and we cannot yet vouch for all parameter values. It is provided to fix ideas, give a sense of the sort of modeling we want, and surface doubts and disagreements — a framework for discussion and comparison, not a definitive cost estimate. We welcome scrutiny and suggestions.

This model aims to provide a starting framework for evaluators and forecasters to:

  1. Explore uncertainty in cost projections (not to deliver authoritative estimates)
  2. Surface key assumptions about cost drivers — and debate which matter most
  3. Compare scenarios based on technology adoption
  4. Anchor discussion and collaborative refinement — a shared reference point, not a settled answer
